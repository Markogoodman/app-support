BoxCount is an offline tile calculator for iPhone and iPad. Estimate tiles and whole boxes for floors, walls, and backsplashes, with openings, waste allowances, and optional material costs.

iOS / iPadOS 17 or later · US$1.99 upfront · No accounts or subscriptions

From measurements to shopping list

  1. Tap New estimate and name your project.
  2. Add your tile material. Enter its tile size, or choose Box Coverage and use the coverage printed on the packaging.
  3. Add the rectangular areas to tile, subtract any openings, and assign a tile material to each area.
  4. Choose a waste allowance. Add tiles per box if using Tile Size and you want box quantities. Add an optional price per box for cost estimates.
  5. Review the results and use Share shopping list to send a text estimate to your chosen app.

Before buying, check the manufacturer’s package coverage, pattern cuts, breakage, and your installer’s advice. BoxCount provides area-based estimates, not a tile layout or cutting plan.

Common questions

Can I use BoxCount without internet?

Yes. Calculations and saved projects work offline. Sending a shopping list through another app may require internet, depending on the destination you choose.

Why do I see tiles but no box total?

Tile Size can estimate whole tiles from dimensions alone. Enter a positive whole number for tiles per box to calculate whole boxes, or use Box Coverage from the manufacturer’s packaging. A project-wide box total appears once all tile materials have packaging information.

Should I use Tile Size or Box Coverage?

Use Box Coverage when the manufacturer provides coverage per package. Tile Size uses the nominal area of one tile, and optionally the number of tiles per box. It does not calculate individual cuts, grout spacing, or patterned layouts.

How are waste and box quantities calculated?

BoxCount subtracts openings from each surface and groups the remaining areas by tile material. It applies each material’s waste percentage, then rounds the required quantity up to whole tiles or whole boxes when the relevant information is available.

Which units and currencies are supported?

Measurements support feet, inches, meters, and centimeters. You can change display units without re-entering measurements. Optional cost estimates are in US dollars and exclude tax, delivery, and installation.

Why is my estimate incomplete?

Check the highlighted entries. Each area needs valid measurements and an assigned tile material. Every tile material needs an assigned area and valid tile size or box coverage. Openings must not exceed the surface area. Price is optional; packaging is needed only for box quantities.

Where are projects saved? Do they sync?

Projects save locally on this device. BoxCount has no built-in cloud sync. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Use the project actions menu to rename, duplicate, or delete a project.

What should I do if saving or loading fails?

If a save fails, use Retry saving and check that your device has available storage. If a load fails, close and reopen BoxCount. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot if you need its saved projects. If the error continues, email support with the exact message.

Are there subscriptions or extra purchases?

No. BoxCount is a US$1.99 paid download with all functionality included, without subscriptions or in-app purchases. App Store prices may vary by region. Apple handles purchases and refund requests.
