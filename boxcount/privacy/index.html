BoxCount Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

BoxCount works offline. The app does not collect or send your personal information or projects to the developer. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

What this policy covers

This policy describes the BoxCount iPhone and iPad app, provided by Marko Peng, and explains the separate handling of support emails and visits to this support website.

Projects stored on your device

BoxCount saves your projects in the app’s local storage. These include project and material names, surface and opening measurements, tile dimensions, packaging information, waste allowances, optional prices, and project creation and editing dates. Calculations run on your device.

The app has no developer-operated server and no built-in cloud sync. Your device’s backup and restore features may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Those services are handled by Apple.

Information you choose to share

When you use “Share shopping list,” BoxCount passes a text estimate to the Apple share sheet. The text includes your project name, material names, quantities, areas, waste allowances, and any prices included in the estimate. It is shared only through the destination you choose. That app or service handles the information under its own privacy practices.

Purchases and Apple services

BoxCount is a paid download with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple handles the App Store purchase and payment information. The app does not request your payment card details. Apple’s App Store, device diagnostics, and backup services operate separately under your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy practices.

Deleting your projects

Use a project’s actions menu on the project list and choose Delete, then confirm, to remove it from the app’s local project storage. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading an app can retain data. Previously shared copies and device backups are managed separately through the destination service or your device and backup settings. The developer cannot access or recover your local projects.

When you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and whatever message and attachments you choose to send, and uses them to handle your request. Email is processed by the email providers involved. Please share only the details needed to explain the issue and remove private information from screenshots or shopping lists.

This support website

This static website uses no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, or external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for the host’s practices. This website hosting is separate from BoxCount’s offline operation.
